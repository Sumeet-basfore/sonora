# Sonora: Launch Video Plan (Brag Plan)

## 1. Project Overview & Rubric Answers

* **Project Name**: Sonora (v0.2)
* **Tagline**: The Sovereign, Bit-Perfect Audiophile Music Player & Instrument.
* **Core Value**: Sovereign local-first playback with bit-perfect dual-decoder engine, real-time 60fps spectrum visualizer, instant FTS5 + MusicBrainz universal search, millisecond-accurate synced lyrics, and zero cloud lock-in.
* **Target Audience**: Audiophiles, music collectors, power users, and developers who own their music library and refuse streaming compromises.
* **Format**: Landscape (1920x1080, 60fps)
* **Total Duration**: 20.0 seconds
* **Tone**: `cinematic` / `polished` (Obsidian-dark, editorial typography, precise sound waves, glowing electric indigo/amber accents).
* **Audio**: Cinematic driving synth beat with transient impacts, sweep rises, and clean click SFX.

## 2. Storyboard & Beat Breakdown (Total: 20s)

### Scene 1: The Hook (0.0s – 3.5s)
* **Visual**: Obsidian darkness awakens with a pulsing radial glow. Bold serif / modernist typography strikes the screen: *"YOUR MUSIC ISN'T A STREAM."* An animated 64-band FFT spectrum analyzer powers up at 60fps across the bottom.
* **Secondary Text**: *"It's an instrument."*
* **Motion**: Fast scale-in with exponential deceleration (expo.out), glowing soundwave ripples.
* **SFX**: Deep sub-bass drop + high-frequency power-on chirp.

### Scene 2: The Audiophile Engine (3.5s – 8.0s)
* **Visual**: Sonora Audiophile Studio workspace UI slides in with smooth 3D perspective tilt. Spotlight on lossless Daft Punk playback, 384kHz 32-bit bit-perfect badge, gapless handover indicator, and live spectrum visualization.
* **Key Callouts (Badges)**:
  * `Bit-Perfect DSP`: Dual-decoder gapless handover (Symphonia + Rubato 64-bit sinc resampler).
  * `EBU R128 / ReplayGain 2.0`: True Peak Limiter with headroom safety.
* **Motion**: Camera pans over the high-res playback transport and audio spectrum analyzer.
* **SFX**: Crisp click, tape-start swell, clean rhythmic beat drop.

### Scene 3: Universal Search & Metadata Enrichment (8.0s – 12.5s)
* **Visual**: `Cmd + K` Universal Command Palette sweeps open instantaneously. User types *"Daft Punk"*:
  * Sub-5ms SQLite FTS5 instant local results.
  * Live MusicBrainz metadata candidates with ranked score meter (98% confidence).
  * Side-by-side Match Inspector and Multi-Provider Artwork Finder (Cover Art Archive, Wikidata, Fanart.tv).
* **Headline**: *"Instant Local Search. Zero-Trust Online Enrichment."*
* **Motion**: Snappy typing simulation, candidate cards cascading into place with glow highlight.
* **SFX**: Mechanical keyboard typing SFX + whoosh sweep.

### Scene 4: Millisecond Synced Lyrics & Dynamic Workspaces (12.5s – 16.5s)
* **Visual**: Transitions into Atmospheric Theater workspace. Large cinematic album artwork on the left, synchronized line-by-line lyrics tracking playback position on the right (`Cmd + L`), and precision timing offset stepper (`±10ms`).
* **Headline**: *"Millisecond Synced Lyrics. 4 Modular Workspaces."*
* **Badges**: `LRCLIB Synced` • `Atmospheric Theater` • `Modern Curator` • `Minimal Player`
* **Motion**: Fluid active lyric glow jump, smooth layout transformation.
* **SFX**: High-tech interface chime, smooth bass glide.

### Scene 5: Outro & Call to Action (16.5s – 20.0s)
* **Visual**: Minimal Sonora logomark with glowing sound ring. Bold closing statement:
  * *"SONORA v0.2"*
  * *"Local-First. Bit-Perfect. Open Source."*
  * *"github.com/Sumeet-basfore/sonora"*
* **Motion**: Clean logo pulse, subtle grain background, final fade to black.
* **SFX**: Final sustained cinematic synth chord + clean reverb tail.
